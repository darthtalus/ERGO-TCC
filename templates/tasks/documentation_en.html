1. Purpose and scope

ERGO combines task management, daily effectiveness, and Environment/Access, Cognition, and Behavior. Ergonomic analysis and workflow adds charts, rule-based recommendations, collaboration, review, templates, recurrence, and sensors.

Notice: recommendations, preventive scores, critical dimensions, and executor suggestions are heuristics. They are not clinical diagnoses, ergonomic reports, or automated employment decisions.

2. Profiles and roles

Profile/roleMain capability
OwnerCreates the task, manages roles, and reviews other executors.
ExecutorControls their assignment, answers checklists, and completes with self-assessment.
StakeholderFollows and evaluates other executors.
ADMIN/SUPERADMIN/superuserOrganization scope, without permission to review their own delivery.
External userOnly sees the public-token page and may request participation.

3. Guarded workflow

DOING ⇄ PAUSED
DOING/PAUSED → CANCELED → DOING
DOING/PAUSED + 1–5 self-assessment → DONE
DONE + RETURNED → DOING
DONE + APPROVED → FINAL
  1. Status belongs to an executor assignment, not the whole task.
  2. DONE cannot be selected from generic status or Kanban.
  3. Completion stores assessment and state in one transaction.
  4. Nobody evaluates or reviews their own assignment.
  5. A return opens a new cycle; approval is final.

4. Cycles and audit

Every review stores decision, reviewer, comment, timestamp, cycle number, and an immutable snapshot of the assessments at that point. Reviews are append-only. After approval, services and screens block changes or deletion of final assessments.

Django Admin protects assignments with history, assessments, and reviews so it cannot be used as a workflow bypass.

5. Dimensions and effectiveness

  • Environment/Access: external conditions and resources.
  • Cognition: focus, mental workload, and processing.
  • Behavior: routine, breaks, posture, and pace.
  • Effectiveness: perceived result recorded separately.

Custom aspects improve flexibility but reduce comparability. Research should use a fixed instrument, scale, and response guide.

6. Analytics and charts

The dashboard has a personal scope and an authorized organization scope. It displays averages, states, pending reviews, divergences, readings, and a rule-based summary.

  • time series;
  • ABC average bars;
  • state doughnut;
  • weekday effectiveness;
  • difficulty histogram;
  • difficulty × effectiveness scatter plot.

Descriptive visualization does not establish causality or validate the theoretical model.

7. Collaboration, checklists, and attachments

  • comments up to 2,000 characters;
  • allowed attachments up to 10 MB;
  • private storage and authorized download;
  • operational checklist copied from templates;
  • preventive checklist only by the executor in DOING/PAUSED;
  • review per assignment.

8. Templates and recurrence

Templates store name, description, duration, difficulty, and one checklist item per line. Rules may be daily, weekly, or monthly; monthly recurrence keeps its anchor day where possible.

python manage.py generate_recurring_tasks

Schedule the command in production. The database records every rule/date occurrence to prevent duplicates.

9. Sensors and API

Records temperature, humidity, noise, luminosity, air quality, source, and note.

POST /upgrades/api/sensors/
X-ERGO-API-Key: ergo_KEY

The full key is shown once; only prefix and hash are stored. Use HTTPS, rate limiting, and revocation.

10. Reports and exports

  • analytical PDF with metrics and charts;
  • original system CSVs;
  • additional pseudonymized CSV, not irreversible anonymization.

11. Security and privacy

  • server-side authorization and CSRF;
  • destructive operations through POST;
  • database constraints;
  • private attachments;
  • public links hide names by default;
  • request status is not disclosed by email;
  • Django superusers work without manual custom-role promotion.

12. Tests and validation

The release includes 43 automated tests and three independent validators. On Windows:

.\scripts\run_validation_windows.ps1

To validate a clean distribution copy, use .\scripts\run_package_validation_windows.ps1.

The deployment environment must record automated and manual acceptance results before production use.

13. Known limitations

  • no drag-and-drop Kanban;
  • recurrence needs an external scheduler;
  • general APIs use Django sessions;
  • no built-in antivirus or rate limiting;
  • sensors are not calibrated by ERGO;
  • heuristics are not a validated scientific instrument.
